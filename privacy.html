TalentTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

TalentTrack helps students prepare for placements and job interviews.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, TalentTrack may collect account details such as your name and email, profile and education details, skills, uploaded resumes, job descriptions, and placement preparation activity.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide account access, analyze resumes against job descriptions, identify skill gaps, provide preparation recommendations, and support app functionality.

Third-Party Services

TalentTrack uses Firebase services for app functionality and may send resume or job-description content to Google's Gemini AI service to provide analysis when you use that feature. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security and Retention

We rely on service-provider security measures and take reasonable steps to protect information. Information is retained only as needed to provide the service or meet applicable legal and security requirements.

Your Choices and Deletion Requests

You may stop using the app and request deletion of your account and associated data using the account deletion instructions linked from the Google Play listing.

Children's Privacy

TalentTrack is intended for students preparing for placements. It is not specifically designed for children under 13.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the support email listed on the TalentTrack Google Play listing.
