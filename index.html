TalentTrack Account Deletion

TalentTrack users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to the TalentTrack support email address listed on Google Play.
  2. Use the subject: TalentTrack Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your TalentTrack account.
  4. Your request will be verified before deletion is processed.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the data stored by the app, the deletion request may cover account information, profile details, uploaded resumes, and placement preparation data.

Data Retention

Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Contact

For account deletion requests, contact the support email listed on the TalentTrack Google Play listing.
