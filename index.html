TalentTrack Account Deletion

Users of TalentTrack can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email your account email address to the TalentTrack developer using the support email shown on the Google Play listing.
  2. Use the subject: TalentTrack Account Deletion Request.
  3. We will verify your request and contact you if more information is needed.

Data deleted

After a verified request is processed, account information, profile details, resumes, and associated placement-preparation data stored by TalentTrack will be deleted, where applicable.

Data retention

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary and deleted when no longer required. Data processed by third-party services may also be subject to their retention policies.

For account deletion requests, contact the support email listed on the TalentTrack Google Play page.
